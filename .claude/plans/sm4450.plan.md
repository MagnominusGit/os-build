## Decisions

- 40 of 46 ROCKNIX SM4450 kernel patches taken (kernel/patches/README.md "SM4450 port").
  Skipped as already carried: 0016, 0019 (our 0370 is its v2), 0041, 0045-0048. Skipped by
  policy: 0054 (battmgr sysfs rename, breaks every SM8550+ profile), 0034 (aw88166 volume
  inversion, would mute Thor's UCM 0).
- Scoped to SM4450 so the combined kernel keeps other SoCs unchanged: 0286 (A613 SPTPRAC), 0862
  (SoundWire EE0). 0826 written on top of our 0820/0825 (senary MI2S).
- Fleet-wide and owed a HW check elsewhere: 0831 aw88166 PLL (Thor Lite / Odin 3 speakers), 1091
  geni RX restart (an rsinput board's pad + BT), 1063 qmp-combo runtime-PM (posted upstream).
- UCM speaker = software volume (our aw88166 semantics); HP Volume remap as on Odin 3.
- WCN3950 BT: shared `qca/cmbtfw13.*`/`cmnv13s.bin` stay the SM6115 (BTFM 00116) ones; ROCKNIX's
  SM4450 set is 00125. If SM4450 BT fails, give the SM4450 DTS a `firmware-name` (nvm, rampatch)
  pointing at per-SoC copies -- btqca honours it; the NVM variant (s/t) is only known from dmesg.
- Wi-Fi: generic ath10k WCN3990 board-2/firmware-5 from linux-firmware (LINUX_FW.pin); WLAN fw is
  inside wpss.mbn, so no rmtfs/tqftpserv (ROCKNIX does not run them on SM4450 either).
- session.conf: TU_DEBUG=noconform for SM4450 (gen1_low, no hw multiview). Plane rotation as on
  SM8xxx: 1080-wide panels sit under SM4450's 1200-line rotator cap.
- GRUB rotation 90 (ROCKNIX GRUB_ROTATION="90"), bootargs `gpt` only.
